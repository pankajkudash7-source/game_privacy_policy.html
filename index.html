Privacy Policy

App Name: Tap Away Arrows
Package Name: com.aistudio.tapawayarrows.pkzm
Last Updated: September 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Tap Away Arrows ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our mobile application.

Summary: Tap Away Arrows is a puzzle game designed for fun and brain training. We do not collect or store any personally identifiable information (PII) like your name, email, or phone number. Game progress is stored locally on your device. Third-party advertising partners (Google AdMob) may collect anonymous device identifiers to serve ads.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. The App itself does not collect any personally identifying information:

  • Game Data (Offline / Local): Your puzzle progress, unlocked levels, stars, coins, hints, and theme preferences are stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device unless backed up by your operating system.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, sign in, or provide any personal details to play the game.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To keep this game free to play, we display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may automatically collect certain device and non-personal diagnostic information to deliver relevant ads and prevent fraud:

  • Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / GAID)
  • Device brand, model, operating system version, and screen specifications
  • IP address and coarse country/region location
  • Ad performance metrics (impressions, clicks, video completion rates)

3. Permissions Used

The App requests minimal permissions strictly necessary for standard operation:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to connect with Google Mobile Ads to fetch and display ads and reward videos.
  • VIBRATE: Used solely to provide optional tactile haptic feedback during gameplay interactions.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

Tap Away Arrows is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. All advertisements served are configured according to Google Play Developer policies, including family-safe content ratings. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

5. Data Retention and Security

Because we do not operate external servers that store personal data, we do not store your private information. Your local game data remains on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the application.

6. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your region (such as the European Economic Area or California), you have rights regarding your advertising identifier and personalized advertising preferences:

  • You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time through your Android device settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID / Delete advertising ID.
